Travel Guide

Everything for the perfect trip

When to come, how to move, what to know — and three ready-made routes through the Kingdom.

Best time to visit

Spring and autumn are the sweet spots — warm days, soft light, fewer crowds.

  • Mar–May: wildflowers in the Atlas, ideal medinas
  • Sep–Nov: harvest season, gentle heat
  • Dec–Feb: ski Oukaïmeden, escape to the warm south
  • Jun–Aug: head to the coast — Essaouira & Agadir

Getting around

Morocco moves easily — fast trains link the north, grand taxis fill the gaps.

  • Al Boraq: Africa's first high-speed line, Tangier–Casa
  • ONCF trains connect Rabat, Fez, Marrakech
  • CTM & Supratours buses for the south
  • Rent a 4×4 for the Atlas & desert pistes

Good to know

A few graces smooth every journey through the Kingdom.

  • Dirham (MAD) is the currency; carry small notes
  • Arabic & Amazigh official; French & English widely spoken
  • Dress modestly in medinas & religious sites
  • Bargaining in souks is expected — and friendly
